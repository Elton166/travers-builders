About the founder

Meet Travers

Travers started Travers Builders in 2000 and still leads the company today. He built it around one idea: a client should be able to deal with one team for the whole job, from the foundation to the finish.

Under his direction the company takes on new houses and renovations, electrical work for industrial, commercial and domestic clients, plumbing, swimming pools, pavements, decks, ceilings and tiling. The work is for homes and businesses in Cape Town and the surrounding areas, and the team is available around the clock.

Travers works on site with his crew, and he puts it simply: your project is his priority.
